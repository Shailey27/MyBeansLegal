02Description of Service

Bean Book is a coffee journal that lets you:

  • Track and organize your coffee bean collection, tastings, scores, and brewing recipes
  • Scan coffee bag labels with your camera to fill in bean details automatically
  • Analyze flavors with an SCA-style flavor wheel and build a personal palate profile
  • Discover and import beans from coffee roasters through integrated search and browsing
  • Use on-device AI features — the AI Dial-In Assistant, Bag Scan, and Puck Diagnosis (Bean Book Pro)
  • Use widgets, Siri, and Shortcuts to log and look up beans
  • Store and manage all of your coffee data locally on your device
